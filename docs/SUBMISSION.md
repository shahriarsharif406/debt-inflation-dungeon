## "Making FinLit Fun" write-up (for the itch.io submission box)

Debt & Inflation Dungeon turns the two forces that quietly drain every budget, interest and inflation, into things you can see. Debt is a monster that grows by a visible percentage every month. Your cash melts as prices rise. Each month you set a budget (needs first, lifestyle, pay yourself first), fight debt with the highest rate first, build an emergency fund, and decide whether a tempting purchase, a raise, a skill course or an investment is worth it. The same rules apply at low, middle and high income, because the debt trap does not care how much you earn. Nothing is taught by a quiz: every idea is a choice with a consequence. Twenty real economic ideas (compound interest, opportunity cost, Fisher's real returns, Markowitz diversification, Kahneman and Tversky's behavioural bias, consumption smoothing, the hedonic treadmill, the Rule of 72, Taleb's black swans, Becker's human capital, rational choice, and eight more including Keynes, Thaler, Modigliani, Akerlof and Stiglitz, Arrow) are practised in play and collected in a Money Codex. Clear your debt and a rocket reaches escape velocity. An optional Gulf pack adds remittances, pay-in-4 instalments, hidden transfer margins and giving season for the UAE and Arab region. Balance is checked with thousands of automated games, and a Daily Dungeon gives everyone the same run to compare.

Debt monsters grow, cash melts, a rocket to escape. Twenty real economic ideas inside.

## Design choices backed by research

A 2026 scoping review of 91 financial-education games (Suna and Aprea, University of Mannheim) found that few state learning objectives, that credit cards and insurance are rarely covered, and that reflection and real-life transfer are not assessed. This game answers each gap:

- **Stated objectives:** the Help screen lists what you practise (budgeting, avalanche, emergency fund, good vs bad debt, lifestyle creep, inflation).
- **Credit cards and insurance are core mechanics**, not side topics.
- **Reflection and transfer:** the end screen gives "What could have been", a "Try it in real life" action and a "Talk about it" question chosen from your own weakest habit.
- **No ads, no purchases, no violence.**
