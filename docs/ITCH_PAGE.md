## Description

**Debt grows. Cash melts. Your move.**

Every debt is a monster that grows by a visible percentage every month. Every dollar you hold buys a little less each month. You have 12 months, a paycheck and a budget. Pay the fastest-growing monster first, build an emergency fund, borrow only when it pays you back, and resist the temptations everyone around you gives in to.

**What you practise (as mechanics, not text):**
- Budgeting: needs, lifestyle, pay yourself first
- Emergency fund and insurance against surprise bills
- Good debt vs bad debt (a cheap skill-course loan against a 4%-a-month card)
- Lifestyle creep when your pay rises
- Investing against inflation: steady, volatile and a space-tech Orbit Fund
- Twenty-one real economic ideas in the Money Codex, from compound interest to human capital

**Features**
- Three income levels: low, middle and high. Rich or poor, the debt trap works the same way
- Daily Dungeon: the same run for everyone today
- Gulf pack (UAE): amounts in AED, rent cheques, end-of-service gratuity, remittance fees, pay-in-4, zakat, and an interest-free family loan (qard hasan) next to the 4% card
- Accessibility panel: text size, high contrast, colour-blind-safe colours, reduce motion, sound captions, plain-language mode, full keyboard play
- Full Arabic interface with right-to-left layout (button on the title screen)
- Report card with "what could have been", XP, levels and a share image
- Plays in the browser on desktop and phone, no install, no account, no personal data

*A simplified model for learning ideas. Not financial advice.*

Made for Zoud GameLab 2026 (Make FinLit Fun).

## Controls
Tap or click. Keyboard: 1 to 9 pay a monster (Shift = pay all), I insurance, Enter or E end month, ? help.
